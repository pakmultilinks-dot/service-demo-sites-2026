Services

Full-service plumbing for Denver homes

From a dripping faucet to a full sewer replacement, every service comes with upfront pricing and a tidy, on-time crew.

Drain Cleaning

Slow sink, gurgling tub, or a main line that backs up every few months. We clear it with pro-grade augers and hydro jetting, then camera-check the line so you know it is actually fixed.

  • ✓ Kitchen, bath and laundry drains
  • ✓ Main sewer line clearing
  • ✓ Camera inspection included

Water Heaters

No hot water, rusty water, or a tank past its prime. We repair all major brands and install tank and tankless units sized to your household, usually in a single visit.

  • ✓ Tank and tankless installs
  • ✓ Same-day replacement available
  • ✓ Old unit hauled away

Leak Detection and Repair

Mystery water bill or a damp spot that keeps coming back. Acoustic sensors and thermal imaging pinpoint the leak without tearing your walls apart first.

  • ✓ Non-invasive leak locating
  • ✓ Slab and wall leak repair
  • ✓ Pipe rerouting options

Fixture Installations

New faucet, toilet, shower valve or disposal. We install customer-supplied or our recommended fixtures, seal everything properly, and test twice before we leave.

  • ✓ Faucets, toilets, showers
  • ✓ Garbage disposals
  • ✓ Supply line upgrades

Sewer Line Services

Recurring backups or soggy spots in the yard. Camera inspection shows exactly what is wrong, then we fix it with spot repair or trenchless replacement that spares your landscaping.

  • ✓ Video camera inspections
  • ✓ Trenchless pipe replacement
  • ✓ Root intrusion removal

Remodel Plumbing

Bathroom or kitchen remodel. Rough-ins, fixture relocations and final trim-outs coordinated with your contractor so the project stays on schedule.

  • ✓ Rough-in and trim-out
  • ✓ Gas line work for ranges
  • ✓ Permit-ready documentation
