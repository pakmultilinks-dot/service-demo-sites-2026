Our story

Built around a smoker and a street corner.

SmokeStack started with a simple idea: great BBQ should not need a reservation. We put a real offset smoker on wheels and started serving the Portland metro one plate at a time.

Everything is cooked the slow way. Wood fire, patience, and recipes we have refined over years of backyard cooks and street service. When the truck sells out, we sell out, because we would rather run out than cut corners.
