Our story

SmokeStack BBQ Truck started with a secondhand offset smoker, a borrowed trailer, and a stubborn belief: great barbecue should come to the people, not the other way around. We smoke brisket, ribs, and chicken low and slow through the night, then roll out to Portland's pods, breweries, and neighborhoods. The menu stays short on purpose. Everything on it earned its place.

"We would rather do a short menu perfectly than a long menu halfway."
