About

Artistry with intention.

GlamCraft Artistry is a New York-based makeup studio serving brides, creatives, and event clients across the metro. Our philosophy is simple: makeup should enhance, never mask. Every look starts with skin prep and a conversation about how you want to feel, not just how you want to look.

We specialize in looks that last. Long-wear, sweat-resistant, camera-tested products mean your makeup looks as good at midnight as it did at noon. From soft, barely-there bridal glam to bold editorial statements, each application is customized to your features, coloring, and the lighting you will be in.

On-location service means the studio comes to you, with professional lighting, a full kit, and a calm presence on busy mornings.

Skin-first, always

Great makeup starts with great prep. We never skip it.
