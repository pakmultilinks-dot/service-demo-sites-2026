Our story

Crumb and Craft opened its ovens with a simple idea: bake everything from scratch, every single morning, and never cut a corner. Our sourdough ferments for two days. Our croissants laminate over three. It takes longer, and you can taste the difference. We are a neighborhood bakery first, and proud of it.

"We would rather do a short menu perfectly than a long menu halfway."
