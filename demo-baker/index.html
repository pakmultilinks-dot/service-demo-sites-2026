Our story

Flour, water, salt, patience.

Crumb & Craft began with a home starter, a secondhand mixer, and a belief that Boston deserves bread worth walking for. Today we bake through the night so the case is full by morning.

We ferment slowly, use honest ingredients, and never rush a loaf. It is a small bakery with a simple promise: everything from scratch, every single day.
