Our story

Roofers who tell you the truth.

PeakShield Roofing Co. is a Dallas-Fort Worth roofing company that started with a frustration: too many homeowners being sold roofs they did not need. We inspect first, document with photos, and recommend only what your roof actually needs.

When a repair will honestly add years of life, we say so. When a replacement is the smart money, we show you why with real documentation. Our crews are local, our cleanup is thorough, and our word is good.
