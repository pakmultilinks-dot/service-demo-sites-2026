Our story

GatherRound started as a supper club among friends who loved feeding people. The dinners got bigger, the guest lists got longer, and eventually we traded the dining room for a full catering kitchen. Today we cater weddings, corporate events, and private parties across Las Vegas with the same philosophy we started with: seasonal food, polished but invisible service, and tables people remember.

"We would rather do a short menu perfectly than a long menu halfway."
