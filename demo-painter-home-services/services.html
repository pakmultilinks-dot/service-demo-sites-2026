Know your sheen

Paint finishes compared

Matte

No shine Best for: Bedrooms, ceilings

Hides wall flaws best. Less washable, so skip it in kid zones.

  • ✓Zero glare, rich color
  • ✓Best at hiding imperfections
  • ✓Touch-ups blend easily

Eggshell

Soft glow Best for: Living rooms, dining rooms

The all-rounder: a hint of sheen with good washability.

  • ✓Subtle, elegant sheen
  • ✓Wipes clean reasonably well
  • ✓Most popular for walls

Satin

Silky sheen Best for: Kitchens, baths, halls

Stands up to moisture and scrubbing. Shows flaws more than matte.

  • ✓Moisture resistant
  • ✓Very washable
  • ✓Great for busy areas

Semi-Gloss

Noticeable shine Best for: Trim, doors, cabinets

Hard, durable finish for woodwork that gets touched daily.

  • ✓Hardest, most durable
  • ✓Highlights crisp trim lines
  • ✓Easy to wipe down
