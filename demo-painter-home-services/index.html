Our story

Painters who treat your home like a canvas.

FreshHue Painting Co. is a Chicago-metro painting company with a simple philosophy: prep is everything. We spend the time on masking, patching, and priming so the finish looks incredible for years, not months.

Our crews are tidy, friendly, and local. Furniture gets covered, floors get protected, and every day ends with a full cleanup. You will barely know we were there, except for the beautiful walls.
