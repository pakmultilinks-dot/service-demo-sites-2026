Our story

Prep-obsessed painters

FreshHue Painting Co. is a demo concept for a residential and commercial painting company serving Chicago. Great paint jobs are 80 percent prep, so the demo leads with the unglamorous part: washing, scraping, sanding, caulking, priming.

Then comes the fun part: premium paints, sharp lines and colors tested on your walls in your light. You approve the color on a sample board before a single roller touches the wall.
