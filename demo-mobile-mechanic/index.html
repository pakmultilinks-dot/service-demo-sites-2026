Our story

Mechanics, minus the waiting room.

WrenchWay Mobile Auto Repair started with a simple frustration: getting a car fixed meant losing half a day to a waiting room. So we put the shop in a van.

Today our certified mobile mechanics travel the Houston metro with professional-grade tools and diagnostic equipment, handling most repairs wherever your car is parked. You get dealership-level work with driveway-level convenience, and a price you approve before we start.

No invented history here: just honest auto repair, done where you are.
