About

Wellness, without the commute.

EaseAway Mobile Massage was built on a simple observation: the best part of a massage is the deep relaxation at the end, and the worst part is driving home through traffic right after. So we come to you.

We serve Seattle and surrounding suburbs with licensed, insured mobile massage. Every appointment includes a professional table, fresh linens, premium oils, and calming music. All you need is a quiet room about the size of a parking space, and we handle everything else.

Whether you need regular deep tissue work for a demanding job, gentle prenatal care, or a one-time reset after a stressful month, sessions are tailored to your body and your day.

"Relaxation should not require a reservation and a road trip."
