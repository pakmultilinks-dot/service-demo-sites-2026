Our story

A salon built for nervous pets.

Pampered Paws is a neighborhood grooming salon built for anxious pets and particular owners. One-on-one appointments, gentle handling, and honest communication, so you always know exactly what happened during your pet's visit.

  • 🐾No cages, no chaos. Pets are groomed one at a time in a calm, quiet space.
  • 🐾Comfort first. If a pet needs a break, they get one. We never rush a scared animal.
  • 🐾You stay in the loop. Coat condition, skin notes, and nail health reported after every visit.
