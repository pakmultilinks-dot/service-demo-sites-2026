Our story

A demo concept done properly

Pampered Paws Grooming is a demo website concept for a cage-free Minneapolis salon built on a simple belief: a groom should feel like a spa day, not an ordeal.

Each pet gets one dedicated groomer from drop-off to pickup, a calm drying process with no cages, and photo updates texted to you mid-groom.

Dogs, cats, nervous rescues, and wiggly puppies all get the same thing: patience, skill, and a really good haircut.
