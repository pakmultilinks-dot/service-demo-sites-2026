Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

A maintenance detail usually takes a few hours. Paint correction and ceramic coating are multi-day jobs because each stage needs proper curing time. We give you an exact timeline with your quote.

Light scratches and swirls in the clear coat can usually be polished out with paint correction. Deep scratches that reach the paint or primer may need touch-up or repainting, and we will tell you honestly which ones those are.

Yes, we offer mobile service across the Miami metro for most detailing packages. We bring water, power, and shade. Ceramic coating and multi-stage correction are done at our studio for controlled conditions.

We hand you a simple care sheet: hand wash with the two-bucket method, avoid automatic brush washes, and dry with clean microfiber. Follow it and the gloss lasts.

Cars, SUVs, trucks, and most exotics. If you have something unusual, call (555) 287-6103 and we will tell you straight whether it is a fit.

Ceramic coatings carry the manufacturer warranty, and our application is backed by a workmanship guarantee. We register the coating and give you the paperwork at pickup.
