The concept

Detailing as a craft, not a car wash

Showroom Shine is a demo concept for drivers who notice the details: the edge of the wheel, the stitching in the seat, the depth of the paint in direct sun.

The concept pairs a boutique studio for correction and coating work with mobile crews for maintenance details, so the car never has to sit in a tunnel wash again.

This website is a design sample showing how that service could present itself online. Every number, review, and price on this page is illustrative.
