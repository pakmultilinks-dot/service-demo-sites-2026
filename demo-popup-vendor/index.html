Our story

Small sellers, big weekends.

PopShop Collective started as a single folding table at a Charlotte block party. We watched neighbors discover a local candle maker, a vintage reseller, and a tamale stand, all in one afternoon, and thought: this should happen every month.

Now we run curated markets across the metro where independent sellers get real foot traffic and shoppers get something they cannot find at the mall. Every market is juried, every vendor is local, and every event is built to be fun first.
