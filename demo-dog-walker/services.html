Services

Dog Walker services for San Francisco

Upfront sample pricing and tidy, on-time crews on every job.

Solo Walks

One-on-one walks for dogs who prefer their own pace, reactive pups, or seniors who like it slow and sniffy.

  • ✓ 30, 45, or 60 minutes
  • ✓ Same walker every visit
  • ✓ Ideal for reactive or senior dogs

Group Adventure Walks

Small groups of four or fewer hit the parks and trails for two hours of social, off-leash fun.

  • ✓ Max 4 dogs per walker
  • ✓ Park and trail routes
  • ✓ Temperament-matched groups

Puppy Visits

Midday potty breaks, playtime, and basic training reinforcement for pups under a year.

  • ✓ Potty break and play
  • ✓ Feeding if needed
  • ✓ Training reinforcement

Drop-in Check-ins

Quick home visits for feeding, fresh water, medication, and a little companionship.

  • ✓ Feeding and fresh water
  • ✓ Medication given as directed
  • ✓ Photo proof of visit
