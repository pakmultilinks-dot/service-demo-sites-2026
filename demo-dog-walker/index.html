Our story

Built for dogs that need more.

TrailBuddy started when our founder's border collie made it clear that a 15-minute loop around the block was not cutting it. So we built the walking service we wished existed: real trails, real exercise, and total transparency for the owner back at the office.

Our walkers know the city's best dog-friendly trails, carry first-aid kits and plenty of water, and treat every dog like their own. You get the route, the photos, and one very tired, very happy dog.

Common questions

Trail talk.

Solo walks run 30 or 60 minutes. Group trail hikes run 60 to 90 minutes including drive time to the trailhead, and weekend adventure hikes are half-day outings. Every walk is GPS-tracked so you see the real distance.

Yes, we walk rain or shine, and we towel dogs off at the door afterward. In truly dangerous weather like lightning or extreme heat, we will contact you to reschedule or switch to an indoor play visit.

Groups are capped at four dogs, matched by size, energy, and temperament. Reactive or anxious dogs are always offered solo walks instead.

Every outing ends with a report card: GPS route map, distance, duration, photos, and notes on water, potty, and behavior. For longer hikes, your walker may send mid-hike photo updates too.

We serve San Francisco and nearby Peninsula and East Bay neighborhoods listed below. Weekend adventure hikes pick up from anywhere in our service area.

Life happens. Cancel or reschedule by 8 PM the night before at no charge. Same-day cancellations are billed at the full walk rate since your walker held the slot.
