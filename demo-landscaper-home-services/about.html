GreenScape Lawn Care is a demo concept for a lawn and garden service serving Raleigh, North Carolina. Clay soil, fescue summers and pine straw: local yards have local quirks, and the demo is built around them.

Weekly routes keep lawns striped and edged, while bed crews handle planting, mulch and cleanups. You get a text before each visit and a photo after, so you always know the yard is handled.
