Check tread depth with a penny: if you can see the top of Lincoln's head, it is time. Also watch for cracking sidewalls, bulges, or vibration. We do free inspections if you are unsure.

Punctures in the tread area can usually be repaired properly with a plug-patch from the inside. Damage to the sidewall or shoulder means the tire must be replaced, and we will show you why.
